Gesture reference

Live while camera is on

Play / Pause

Bring both hands together in front of the camera, like a clap.

Volume

Pinch thumb and index finger, then slide up or down.

Next track

Sweep an open hand quickly from left to right.

Previous track

Sweep an open hand quickly from right to left.

Mute / Unmute

Hold up a peace sign — index and middle finger extended.

Like track

Thumbs up, with the rest of the hand closed.

Play / Pause (clap)

One sharp clap near your device's mic. Works even with the camera off.

Video never leaves your device — hand tracking runs locally in the browser. Add GestureFlow to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.
